GetNewWord 1.4.1：修好 1.4.0 升级后搬牌组的一个问题。词库里有些词的卡在 Anki 里已经删掉时，1.4.0 一张都没搬；1.4.1 跳过这些卡，其余的照常搬进新牌组。

## 下载

| 系统 | 文件 |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片（macOS 14 或更新） | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.4.1.dmg` 和它是同一个文件，带版本号方便存档。已经装着 1.2.0 到 1.4.0 的会自动收到这一版的更新提示，不用手动下载。

第一次安装：打开 DMG，把 GetNewWord 拖进「应用程序」，再从「应用程序」里打开。安装包签了 Developer ID，但没有经过苹果公证，所以第一次打开时 macOS 会提示无法验证、不让打开。关掉提示，打开 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，拉到最下面点 **仍要打开**，再确认一次（可能要输入开机密码）。只有第一次要这样，之后自动更新装上的新版不会再拦。

## 这一版改了什么

- 升级后第一次连上 Anki 搬牌组时，本地还记着、Anki 里已经删掉的卡会跳过，不再让整次搬家失败。用 1.4.0 没搬成的，装上 1.4.1 后下次连上 Anki 会接着搬：知识片段进「知识」，补上「来自::」「主题::」标签，空了的老分类牌组删掉。
- 日志里卡片样式的版本号写对了（v3）。

1.4.0 的改动（Anki 单词卡句子在前、和 Ask Free、ChatCards 同一种卡、按语言分牌组）见 [1.4.0 的发布说明](https://github.com/SchaeferAnjon/GetNewWord/releases/tag/v1.4.0)。

## English

GetNewWord 1.4.1 fixes moving cards after the 1.4.0 update. When some words in the library still pointed to cards already deleted in Anki, 1.4.0 moved none of the cards; 1.4.1 skips those and moves the rest into the new decks.

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple silicon and Intel (macOS 14 or later) | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.4.1.dmg` is the same file with the version in its name. If you have 1.2.0 to 1.4.0, the update is offered automatically.

First install: open the DMG, drag GetNewWord into Applications and open it from there. The app is signed with Developer ID but not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify the app and will not open it. Close the message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, click **Open Anyway** and confirm (you may be asked for your login password). You only do this once; updates installed automatically are not blocked.

**What changed**

- When Anki is first connected after the update, cards the library still remembers but that were deleted in Anki are skipped instead of making the whole move fail. If 1.4.0 did not move your cards, 1.4.1 finishes the move the next time Anki is connected: knowledge snippets into 知识 with their 来自:: and 主题:: tags, and empty old category decks removed.
- The log shows the right card style version (v3).

For the 1.4.0 changes (sentence-first word cards, one kind of card with Ask Free and ChatCards, one deck per language) see the [1.4.0 release notes](https://github.com/SchaeferAnjon/GetNewWord/releases/tag/v1.4.0).
