GetNewWord 1.5.0：查词面板的透明度可以自己调；不再做使用统计，也不再送免费次数。

## 下载

| 系统 | 文件 |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片（macOS 14 或更新） | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.5.0.dmg` 和它是同一个文件，带版本号方便存档。已经装着 1.2.0 到 1.4.1 的会自动收到这一版的更新提示，不用手动下载。

第一次安装：打开 DMG，把 GetNewWord 拖进「应用程序」，再从「应用程序」里打开。安装包签了 Developer ID，但没有经过苹果公证，所以第一次打开时 macOS 会提示无法验证、不让打开。关掉提示，打开 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，拉到最下面点 **仍要打开**，再确认一次（可能要输入开机密码）。只有第一次要这样，之后自动更新装上的新版不会再拦。

## 这一版改了什么

- **查词面板透明度**：设置 › 翻译与学习 最下面多了「面板透明度」，30% 到 100%，默认 100%。调低了能透过面板看到后面的字幕和网页。钉住的面板松手就变，没钉住的下次弹出时用新值。
- **打开设置不再套蓝框**：以前一打开设置，「通用」外面有一圈蓝色焦点框；方案和价格窗口一打开，授权码输入框也套着蓝框。现在都没有了。
- **不做使用统计**：不再每天发匿名统计，也不再问同不同意，欢迎引导从六步变成五步；登录同步账号后也不再报「登录」「打开」。
- **不再送免费次数**：会员只有订阅一种，方案和价格只剩会员和自带 Key 两栏；自带 Key 照旧永久免费、不限次数。以前领到的免费次数还没用完的，照常能用，设置里照实写剩几次。

## English

GetNewWord 1.5.0: you can set how see-through the lookup panel is; the app no longer collects usage statistics or gives free lookups.

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple silicon and Intel (macOS 14 or later) | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.5.0.dmg` is the same file with the version in its name. If you have 1.2.0 to 1.4.1, the update is offered automatically.

First install: open the DMG, drag GetNewWord into Applications and open it from there. The app is signed with Developer ID but not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify the app and will not open it. Close the message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, click **Open Anyway** and confirm (you may be asked for your login password). You only do this once; updates installed automatically are not blocked.

**What changed**

- **Lookup panel opacity**: Settings › Translation now ends with a Panel opacity slider, 30% to 100%, 100% by default. Lower it to see subtitles and pages behind the panel. A pinned panel changes when you let go of the slider; otherwise the next panel uses the new value.
- **No blue ring when settings open**: the first sidebar item no longer has a blue focus ring when Settings opens, and the licence code field no longer has one when Plans & pricing opens.
- **No usage statistics**: the app no longer sends an anonymous daily count or asks about it, so the welcome window has five steps instead of six; signing in to sync no longer reports login and open events.
- **No free lookups**: membership is subscription only, and Plans & pricing shows two columns, membership and your own key. Your own key is still free forever with no limits. Free lookups you already received keep working, and Settings shows how many are left.
