`GetNewWord-1.3.0.dmg` 和它是同一个文件，带版本号方便存档。已经装着 1.2.0 的会自动收到这一版的更新提示，不用手动下载。

## 这一版改了什么

**会员**

- 会员开放了，不用自己申请 API Key：欢迎引导里选会员，或以后在 **设置 → AI 服务** 里选。前 100 位新用户免费 30 次，不限期；之后每月 $1.99 或每年 $19.90（人民币每月 ¥9.9、每年 ¥99），每天最多 200 次，北京时间早上 8 点重新计。人民币还可以用微信一次买 1 个月或 1 年，到期就停，不自动续费。
- 一个授权码最多能用在 2 台 Mac 上。按月、按年的订阅在 **设置 → AI 服务 → 管理会员 → 管理订阅** 里换卡、改月付年付、取消、看发票；换了电脑或没装软件，用官网和 README 里的登录链接，填付款时的邮箱。
- 不用注册账号：第一次用会员时才登记这台 Mac 并领试用。没领到试用、到了每天上限、付款没完成、订阅管理打不开，都会写清原因。
- 自带 Key 仍然永久免费、没有次数限制，用哪家 AI 都行。
- 多台 Mac 之间同步词库是会员功能（在 **设置 → 账号与同步** 用 Google 登录）。1.2.0 说过已登录的电脑照常同步，从这一版起同步也要是会员。查词、词库、同步到 Anki 都不用账号。

**查词面板**

- 默认样式换回 1.1.x 的经典卡片：带取词截图和红框，内容往下滚。浮签和讲义卡还在，**设置 → 翻译与学习** 里能选。
- 查的词在整张卡里标紫：语境、用法、搭配、例句、词根词源、语法分解和变化那行里，只认整个词（wheat 里的 heat 不算）；变形整个词标（heats、ran、Häuser），短语整块标（gave up），德语可分动词的词头也跟着标（hält … an）。深色模式下紫色亮一档，更好读。
- 框的是变形或带变音的词（hält、ran）时，用法、例句、词根词源不再一直停在「生成中」，词也不会念两遍。
- 面板骨架的词头和结果卡一致，结果回来时分隔线不再往下跳。
- 语法分解里模型写的 **加粗** 不再显示成星号。
- 老词会照实写「已在词库里 · 语境相同，没再存」，不再重复提示。

**更快、更稳**

- 关掉主窗口后软件留在菜单栏，不再整个退出：快捷键照常查词，点菜单栏的 **打开主窗口** 回到关之前那一页那个词。
- 框完词，面板马上弹出来，框里的字在后台认；2000 个词的词库里，按键到面板第一帧从 0.8 秒降到 0.06 秒。
- 冷启动时主线程不再等标紫用的词元模型；打开主窗口、滚词库、换词、搜索打字都不再把整个词库取一遍（2000 个词时，开窗到第一帧 1.8 秒降到 0.7 秒，滚列表每秒掉帧 79 降到 2）。
- Anki 开着时，查完词的同步不再卡住界面，读卡时点关面板也不再被挡。

**修复**

- 从菜单栏菜单里框词、快速查词、同步 Anki 时，词以前只显示「已存进」却没有存下来，Anki 同步也找不到它们。现在都进词库了。
- Anki 同步等回话期间删掉一个已经同步的词，不再闪退。

**设置**

- 截图取词和指词查词的 **多按一个键** 可以自己选 ⌥、⇧、⌃、⌘ 或不用，默认仍是 ⌥；和另一组撞了会标出来。快捷键键帽认得 F13 到 F20。
- 翻译与学习页顶上的示意图跟着选中的面板样式画。
- 英文和德文界面里被截断的几处说明改短了。

## English

GetNewWord 1.3.0: membership is open (no API key needed; the first 100 new users get 30 lookups free), the look-up panel is back to the classic card, the looked-up word is marked purple across the whole card, closing the main window no longer quits the app, and start-up and opening and closing the panel are faster.

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple silicon and Intel (macOS 14 or later) | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.3.0.dmg` is the same file with the version in its name. If you have 1.2.0, the update is offered automatically.

First install: open the DMG, drag GetNewWord into Applications and open it from there. The app is signed with Developer ID but not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify the app and will not open it. Close the message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, click **Open Anyway** and confirm (you may be asked for your login password). You only do this once; updates installed automatically are not blocked.

**What changed**

- **Membership is open.** No API key to set up: pick it in the welcome window or under **Settings → AI service**. The first 100 new users get 30 lookups free, with no expiry; after that $1.99 a month or $19.90 a year (¥9.9 or ¥99 in yuan), up to 200 lookups a day, reset at 8:00 Beijing time. Paying in yuan you can also buy 1 month or 1 year once with WeChat Pay; it ends on its date and does not renew. One licence code works on up to 2 Macs.
- **Manage your subscription** under **Settings → AI service → Manage membership → Manage subscription**: change card, switch monthly and yearly, cancel, invoices. No longer on that Mac? Use the login link on the website or in the README and enter the email you paid with.
- **No sign-up.** The Mac is registered, and the trial given out, only the first time you use membership. When a trial could not be given, the daily limit is reached, a payment did not finish or subscription management would not open, the app now says why.
- **Your own key** is still free forever with no limits, with any provider.
- **Sync between Macs** is for members (log in with Google under **Settings → Account & sync**). 1.2.0 said Macs that were already signed in keep syncing; from this version sync also needs membership. Look-ups, the library and Anki need no account.
- **Classic look-up panel by default**: the card with the capture and its red box, content scrolling down, as in 1.1.x. The tag and lecture card styles are still in **Settings → Translation**.
- **The looked-up word is purple across the whole card**: context, usage, collocations, examples, etymology, grammar breakdown and the inflection line. Whole words only (the heat in wheat is not marked); inflections are marked whole (heats, ran, Häuser), phrases as a block (gave up), and the prefix of a German separable verb with it (hält … an). The purple is a step lighter in dark mode.
- **Fixes in the panel**: usage, examples and etymology no longer stay on "generating" when you frame an inflection or a word with an umlaut (hält, ran), and the word is no longer read twice; the skeleton's header matches the result card so the divider stops jumping; **bold** in the grammar breakdown no longer shows as asterisks; a word already in the library says so once.
- **Closing the main window no longer quits the app.** It stays in the menu bar, shortcuts keep working, and **Open main window** returns to the page and word you left.
- **Faster**: the panel appears as soon as you finish framing and the text is read in the background (with 2000 words, key press to first frame went from 0.8 s to 0.06 s); cold start no longer waits for the lemma model; opening the main window, scrolling, switching words and typing in search no longer load the whole library (with 2000 words, window to first frame 1.8 s to 0.7 s, dropped frames per second when scrolling 79 to 2); the Anki sync after a lookup no longer stalls the interface.
- **Fixed**: words framed or looked up from the menu bar menu showed as saved but were not, and Anki sync could not find them; they are saved now. Deleting an already-synced word while Anki sync was waiting for a reply no longer crashes the app.
- **Settings**: **Add one more key** for capture and point can be ⌥, ⇧, ⌃, ⌘ or none (⌥ by default), clashes are marked, and the shortcut keycaps know F13 to F20. The illustration on the Translation page follows the selected panel style, and a few cut-off lines in English and German are shorter.
