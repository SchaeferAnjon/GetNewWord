GetNewWord 1.4.0：Anki 单词卡改成「句子在前」，正面是你遇到它的那句话，翻过来是这句里的意思，还能加一张语境填空卡；卡片和 Ask Free、ChatCards 做的是同一个样子，单词按语言分牌组，知识片段都进「知识」，分类写成标签。

## 下载

| 系统 | 文件 |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片（macOS 14 或更新） | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.4.0.dmg` 和它是同一个文件，带版本号方便存档。已经装着 1.2.0 或 1.3.0 的会自动收到这一版的更新提示，不用手动下载。

第一次安装：打开 DMG，把 GetNewWord 拖进「应用程序」，再从「应用程序」里打开。安装包签了 Developer ID，但没有经过苹果公证，所以第一次打开时 macOS 会提示无法验证、不让打开。关掉提示，打开 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，拉到最下面点 **仍要打开**，再确认一次（可能要输入开机密码）。只有第一次要这样，之后自动更新装上的新版不会再拦。

## 更新以后 Anki 里会变什么

- 卡片换上新样子：只换模板和样式，复习记录不动，Anki 平常的同步就行。
- 第一次连上 Anki 时搬一次牌组：单词搬进「语言学习::<语言>」（本来就在这里的不动），语言下面再分的类写成「分类::」标签；知识片段搬进「知识」，分类写成「主题::」标签。每张卡带「来自::GetNewWord」。空了的老分类牌组删掉，只删 GetNewWord 自己建过、里面一张卡都没有的。复习记录跟着卡走。
- 单词卡加了「Fill」字段（语境填空用）、知识卡加了「Question」字段（正面的问题）。加字段 Anki 会要你选一次上传或下载，所以 GetNewWord 先问你，按它列的三步做：先在手机上同步一次，再点「继续」，之后在 Anki 里选「上传」。点「以后再说」就先用新样子，不加这两个字段。
- 别的电脑上还没更新的 GetNewWord 同步时，会把知识片段搬回原来的牌组：都更新到 1.4.0 就不会了。
- GetNewWord 不再在送完卡后替你点 Anki 的同步，在 Anki 里照常同步就行。

## 这一版改了什么

**Anki 单词卡：句子在前**

- 正面是你最近遇到这个词的那句话，词套着取景框；没有句子的词，正面是词本身。
- 翻过来，译文接在原句下面，原句的位置不动；下面是这句里的意思（大字）、词头、发音、音标和全部释义。用法、搭配、例句、词源、每次遇到的句子、当时的画面都收进折叠，手机上一屏看得完答案。
- 语境填空卡（可选）：把句子里的词挖掉，看译文填回去。**设置 → Anki** 里选「不加 / 查过 3 次以上的词 / 所有词」，词卡右键也能单独加。关掉以后，已有的填空卡会暂停，再打开就恢复。
- 知识片段的卡正面是一个问题（AI 存片段时一起出），翻过来是要点。
- 卡上的固定字跟界面语言（中文、英文、德文），恢复词库三种写法都认。
- 后台按新拼法重写已同步的卡时，你在 Anki 里改过的文字留着不动。你删掉的「语境填空」卡片类型不会被悄悄加回去。

**三个软件同一种卡**

- 单词卡、知识卡都放在同一张纸上：桌面上浅灰底上一张纸，手机上纸铺满屏幕；深色模式也排好了。和 Ask Free、ChatCards 送进 Anki 的卡是同一个样子。
- 新存的知识片段写进三个软件共用的笔记类型「学习卡 · 问答」；以前的片段留在「GetNewWord Snippet」里，样子一样。
- 牌组只按语言分（「语言学习::英语」），知识片段都进「知识」，分类、主题写成标签，按标签就能筛。你在 Anki 里给「语言学习」「知识」改了名，GetNewWord 跟着新名字走。
- 样式只往新的换：另一台电脑上的 GetNewWord 先更新了，这一台的旧版不会把新样子换回去。
- **从 Anki 恢复词库**按「分类::」「主题::」标签认回分类，也能恢复「学习卡 · 问答」里的片段。

**修复**

- 变形的词（stems）在卡上整个词标紫，不再只标 stem。
- 一个词配 6 句以上语境时，句子和译文的顺序不再乱。
- 升级卡片的任何一步出错都只记日志，新卡照样同步。

## English

GetNewWord 1.4.0: the Anki word card now puts the sentence first. The front is the sentence you met the word in, the back is what it means there, and you can add a fill-in card. The cards look the same as those made by Ask Free and ChatCards; words go into one deck per language, knowledge snippets into one deck, and your categories become tags.

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple silicon and Intel (macOS 14 or later) | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.4.0.dmg` is the same file with the version in its name. If you have 1.2.0 or 1.3.0, the update is offered automatically.

First install: open the DMG, drag GetNewWord into Applications and open it from there. The app is signed with Developer ID but not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify the app and will not open it. Close the message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, click **Open Anyway** and confirm (you may be asked for your login password). You only do this once; updates installed automatically are not blocked.

**What changes in Anki after the update**

- The cards get the new look. Only the templates and styling change; your review history stays, and a normal Anki sync is enough.
- The first time Anki is connected, GetNewWord moves the cards once: words into 语言学习::<language> (those already there stay), with the categories under a language written as 分类:: tags; knowledge snippets into 知识, with their categories as 主题:: tags. Every card gets the tag 来自::GetNewWord. Old category decks that end up empty are deleted, only those GetNewWord made itself and only when no card is left in them. Review history moves with the cards.
- Word cards get a Fill field (for the fill-in card) and snippet cards a Question field. Adding fields makes Anki ask for a one-way sync, so GetNewWord asks you first and lists three steps: sync your phone once, click **Continue**, then choose **Upload** in Anki. **Later** keeps the new look without the two fields.
- A GetNewWord on another Mac that is not updated yet moves snippets back to their old decks when it syncs; once all are on 1.4.0 this stops.
- GetNewWord no longer starts Anki's sync after sending cards; sync in Anki as usual.

**What changed**

- **Sentence first.** The front is the sentence you last met the word in, with the word framed; a word without a sentence shows the word. On the back the translation follows the sentence, which stays where it was, then the meaning in this sentence in large type, the word, pronunciation, phonetic spelling and all its meanings. Usage, collocations, examples, word roots, every sentence you met it in and the screenshot are folded, so the answer fits one phone screen.
- **Fill-in cards (optional)** hide the word in the sentence for you to fill back from the translation. Choose None, Words looked up 3+ times, or All words under **Settings → Anki**, or add one for a single word from its context menu. Turning them off suspends the existing fill-in cards; turning them on again resumes them.
- **Snippet cards** ask a question on the front (the AI writes it when the snippet is saved) and show the key points on the back.
- The fixed words on the cards follow the app language (Chinese, English, German), and restoring the library reads all three.
- When synced cards are rewritten in the new format, text you edited in Anki is kept. A fill-in card type you deleted is not added back.
- **One kind of card across three apps.** Word and snippet cards sit on the same sheet: a sheet on a light grey background on the desktop, filling the screen on a phone, with dark mode. They look the same as the cards Ask Free and ChatCards send. New snippets use the note type 学习卡 · 问答 shared by the three apps; earlier snippets stay in GetNewWord Snippet and look the same.
- **Decks.** One deck per language (语言学习::英语), knowledge snippets in 知识, categories and topics as tags you can filter by. Rename 语言学习 or 知识 in Anki and GetNewWord follows the new name.
- **Styling only moves forward**: an older GetNewWord on another Mac does not switch the new look back.
- **Restore from Anki** reads categories back from the 分类:: and 主题:: tags and also restores snippets from 学习卡 · 问答.
- **Fixed**: an inflected word (stems) is marked purple as a whole word; with more than 6 sentences, sentences and translations no longer get out of order; any failed step while upgrading the cards is only logged and new cards still sync.
