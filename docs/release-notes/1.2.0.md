GetNewWord 1.2.0：指着生词按一下就能查，查词面板、主窗口和设置都重做了，支持 Intel 芯片，释义能写成 8 种语言，从这一版起自动更新。

## 下载

| 系统 | 文件 |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片（macOS 14 或更新） | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.2.0.dmg` 和它是同一个文件，带版本号方便存档。打开 DMG，把 GetNewWord 拖进「应用程序」，再从「应用程序」里打开。

安装包签了 Developer ID，但没有经过苹果公证，所以第一次打开时 macOS 会提示无法验证、不让打开。关掉提示，打开 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性**，拉到最下面点 **仍要打开**，再确认一次（可能要输入开机密码）。只有第一次要这样，之后自动更新装上的新版不会再拦。

## 装着 1.1.x 的，这一次要手动装

1.1.x 没有自动更新，1.2.0 又换了签名证书，所以要手动装一次：

1. 在菜单栏的 GetNewWord 图标里点 **退出**。
2. 下载 `GetNewWord.dmg`，把 GetNewWord 拖进「应用程序」，问要不要替换时点 **替换**。
3. 打开新版：被拦下时照上面一段，在 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性** 里点 **仍要打开**。
4. 重新给屏幕录制权限：打开 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 录屏与系统录音**（macOS 14 叫「屏幕录制」），列表里已经有 GetNewWord 的，先选中它点 **−** 删掉；再打开 GetNewWord，点 **去授权**，把浮出来的图标拖进列表，打开开关后重开一次 GetNewWord。
5. 第一次查词时如果弹出钥匙串提示，问 GetNewWord 能不能用钥匙串里存的 API Key，输入开机密码后点 **始终允许**。

词库、语境截图、设置和 Anki 牌组都会保留。1.2.0 不再需要辅助功能权限，1.1.x 给过的可以在 **系统设置 → 隐私与安全性 → 辅助功能** 里删掉。以后有新版本会自动提示，不用再手动下载。

## 这一版改了什么

**查词**

- 指词查词：鼠标指着词按 `⌃⌘A`，不用框。默认只查不存，按住 `⌥` 就存进词库；**设置 → 快捷键与取词** 里能反过来。
- 更快：框住的词本机读得出字时只把文字发给 AI，不再让它看图，快 2 到 3 秒；指词查词从按键到出释义约 1.4 秒。
- 查词面板重做，有两种样式：跟着词的浮签（可以默认展开，分页看释义、用法、例句、构词、语法）和讲义卡，在 **设置 → 翻译与学习** 里选。
- 面板不抢键盘：看视频时空格照样暂停和播放，按 Esc 关面板。
- 出错时说白话、给能直接解决的按钮：没填 Key、Key 不对、额度用完（**换一家 AI**、**打开它的后台**）、模型名不对（**去改模型**）、回复被截断、超时、连不上、没认出词。英文和德文界面不再弹中文报错，Gemini 的报错也读得懂了。
- 译文更地道：先照目标语言的习惯译整句，再对齐词义；固定搭配（比如 compound interest）按整个搭配讲；原句已经是目标语言时，改写成更浅白的说法。译文里的高亮不再标错字，音标不再显示成 //…//。

**语言**

- 释义和译文可以写成简体中文、繁體中文、English、Deutsch、Français、Español、日本語、한국어，在设置或主窗口的工具栏里改。
- 界面有简体中文、English、Deutsch 三种，默认跟系统；从 1.1.x 升级的保持中文。

**主窗口**

- 改成三栏：侧栏分类跟着页签走，列表能右键、能用上下键换词，详情分层显示，带语境页签。
- 统计页有每天查词的热力图和反复遇到的词。
- 词库还空着时有三步引导：选 AI、拖图标授权、真的框一次词。

**Anki 和词库**

- Anki 卡片背面的小标题（用法、搭配、例句、词根词源）跟着界面语言写成中文、英文或德文。
- 换了界面语言，没归类的词还进原来那个牌组，不会同一种语言分到两个牌组。
- 导入 CSV 认得引号里的换行和引号，导出再导入不再丢词。

**设置和首次引导**

- 设置窗口重做：左边九页，每页一张会动的示意图；首次引导六步。
- 新加「关于我们」：官网、联系邮箱（点一下就能写信，主题带着版本号）和日志文件夹。
- 「更新与关于」里有使用说明（快捷键跟着你的设置变）、**重新看引导**，还写明了查词时什么会发出去、发给谁。
- 授权更省事：点 **去授权** 会打开系统设置，旁边浮出 GetNewWord 的图标，拖进权限列表就加上并打开开关。只要屏幕录制一项权限，不再要辅助功能。
- 不是从「应用程序」打开的（比如直接在 DMG 里点开）会提示，点 **移过去并重新打开** 就搬好。
- API Key 粘贴后自动验证，通过就存进钥匙串；切到别的设置页也不会丢掉还没验证完的 Key。
- 只在菜单栏常驻，不占 Dock；Dock 图标和菜单栏图标都能在设置里开关。「开机自动打开」现在真的会生效。
- 删掉了从来没起作用的旧设置项（每日复习上限、自动归档、截图质量等），也去掉了只能连作者自己服务的 Atrium 生词收件箱。

**匿名使用统计**

- 我们会给前 100 位用户免费额度，所以需要统计有多少人在用。统计是匿名的，同意了才发：新装的在欢迎引导里问，从 1.1.x 升级的第一次打开主窗口时问。
- 同意后每天发一次：装软件时随机生成的编号、版本号、系统和芯片、用会员还是自带 Key、界面语言。不发你查的词、词库、截图和 Key。随时能在 **设置 → 更新与关于 → 匿名使用统计** 里关掉。

**同步和会员**

- 多台 Mac 之间同步词库改成会员功能。会员还没开放，现在没登录的电脑登录不了；已经登录的照常同步。查词、词库、同步到 Anki 都不用账号，自带 Key 一直免费。

**安装和更新**

- 一个安装包同时支持 Apple 芯片和 Intel 芯片（1.1.x 只有 Apple 芯片版），从 zip 改成了 DMG。
- 自动更新：每天在后台查一次，也可以在菜单栏菜单或 **设置 → 更新与关于** 里点 **检查更新**。
- 代码以 GPL-3.0-or-later 开源。

GetNewWord 1.2.0: point at a word and press a key to look it up; the look-up panel, the main window and Settings are redone; Intel Macs are supported; explanations can be written in 8 languages; and from this version on the app updates itself.

| System | File |
| --- | --- |
| Mac · Apple silicon and Intel (macOS 14 or later) | `GetNewWord.dmg` |

`GetNewWord-1.2.0.dmg` is the same file with the version in its name. Open the DMG, drag GetNewWord into Applications and open it from there.

The app is signed with Developer ID but not notarized by Apple, so the first time you open it macOS says it cannot verify the app and will not open it. Close the message, open **System Settings → Privacy & Security**, scroll to the bottom, click **Open Anyway** and confirm (you may be asked for your login password). You only do this once; updates installed automatically are not blocked.

**Coming from 1.1.x: install this one by hand.** 1.1.x cannot update itself and 1.2.0 is signed with a different certificate:

1. Click the GetNewWord icon in the menu bar and choose **退出** (Quit); 1.1.x is in Chinese only.
2. Download `GetNewWord.dmg`, drag GetNewWord into Applications and click **Replace**.
3. Open the new version; when macOS stops it, click **Open Anyway** as described above.
4. Allow Screen Recording again: in **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Screen & System Audio Recording** (**Screen Recording** on macOS 14), select the old GetNewWord entry and remove it with **−**. Then open GetNewWord, click **Grant access** (**去授权** while the app is still in Chinese), drag the floating icon into the list, switch it on and reopen GetNewWord once.
5. If a Keychain prompt asks whether GetNewWord may use the API key it stored, enter your login password and click **Always Allow**.

Your library, screenshots, settings and Anki decks stay. 1.2.0 no longer needs the Accessibility permission; you can remove it under **System Settings → Privacy & Security → Accessibility**. From now on new versions are offered automatically.

**What changed**

- **Point to look up**: rest the pointer on a word and press `⌃⌘A`, no framing. It looks up without saving by default; hold `⌥` to save, or reverse this in **Settings → Shortcuts & capture**.
- **Faster**: when the framed word can be read on your Mac, only the text goes to the AI, 2 to 3 seconds faster; pointing takes about 1.4 seconds from key press to meaning.
- **New look-up panel** in two styles, a tag that follows the word and a lecture card (**Settings → Translation**). It never takes the keyboard, so Space still pauses your video; Esc closes it.
- **Errors in plain words**, with a button that fixes them: no key, wrong key, quota used up, wrong model name, cut-off reply, timeout, no connection, no word found. English and German no longer show Chinese error messages.
- **Better translations**: the whole sentence first, the way the target language says it, then the word's sense; fixed expressions explained as a whole.
- **Languages**: explanations in Simplified or Traditional Chinese, English, German, French, Spanish, Japanese or Korean; the app itself in Simplified Chinese, English or German (follows the system; people updating from 1.1.x keep Chinese).
- **Main window** in three columns, a stats page with a heat map, and three first steps while the library is empty.
- **Anki**: the section labels on the back of the card follow the app language (Chinese, English or German). CSV import handles line breaks and quotes inside quotes.
- **Settings** redone: nine pages, each with a moving illustration, a six-step welcome, an **About Us** page, and a list of what is sent where when you look a word up. Only Screen Recording is needed now, and dragging the floating icon into the list grants it. Pasted API keys are checked and stored right away.
- **Anonymous usage statistics**: we give the first 100 users free credit, so we need to count how many people use the app. If you agree, the app sends once a day a random ID made at install, the version, system and chip, membership or your own key, and the app language; never your words, library, screenshots or keys. New users are asked during setup, people updating from 1.1.x the first time they open the main window; change it any time under **Settings → Updates & about → Anonymous usage statistics**.
- **Sync between Macs** is now part of membership, which is not open yet: Macs that are not signed in cannot sign in for now, and Macs already signed in keep syncing. Look-ups, the library and Anki need no account, and your own key stays free.
- **One download for Apple silicon and Intel** (1.1.x was Apple silicon only), as a DMG instead of a zip; it checks for updates once a day, or use **Check for Updates** in the menu bar menu or **Settings → Updates & about**.
- Open source under GPL-3.0-or-later.
